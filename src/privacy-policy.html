Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you provide it, automatically when you use the Services, and from event organizers or authorized parties managing an event or guest experience through VOW. Depending on the services used, this may include:

  • Contact and account information. Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, mailing addresses, organizations, job titles, login credentials, and account preferences.
  • Event and guest information. Invitations, registrations, RSVP responses, guest and group assignments, seating, passes, attendance, and information submitted through event forms.
  • Travel and preferences. Travel details, itineraries, dietary needs, accessibility requirements, and other information provided to support an event or guest experience. Some of this information may be considered sensitive under applicable law.
  • Information about children. Information provided in connection with children participating in an event, including guest details and relevant travel, dietary, or other event requirements.
  • Communications and transactions. Messages, inquiries, support requests, and information needed to facilitate transactions where purchasing functionality is enabled. Payment information is handled through payment processing providers.
  • Technical and usage information. Information about your device, browser, and interactions with the Services.

The information requested for a particular event depends on the organizer and the functionality used. VOW does not collect government-issued identification documents. Please do not submit those documents through the Services.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate the Services; facilitate invitations, registration, seating, passes, check-in, travel coordination, and related communications; support enabled transactions; respond to inquiries; troubleshoot, secure, improve, and personalize the Services; fulfill the purposes for which information was provided; and meet contractual and legal obligations.

Event-related dietary, accessibility, and other sensitive information is provided to support the relevant event requirements. Organizers are responsible for obtaining any notices, permissions, or consents required for the information they request.

Event Organizers and Authorized Users

Business customers use VOW to manage events and guest experiences. Event information may be supplied by an organizer, an authorized collaborator, a guest, or another person authorized to provide it. Organizers determine the information requested and the access granted to their teams and collaborators. Authorized users may access and manage information through the functionality and permissions available to them.

The organizer’s privacy practices apply to its own collection and use of information. Questions about an organizer’s event, communications, or use of guest information should be directed to that organizer. You may also contact VOW for assistance.

Sharing Information

VOW does not sell information, including personal, professional, customer, or guest information.

We may disclose information with your consent or as necessary to provide a service or complete a transaction you request or authorize. This may include the applicable organizer, authorized collaborators, and service providers supporting the Services, such as hosting, communications, support, and payment processing providers.

We may also disclose information when required by law or valid legal process; to protect our customers or other individuals; to maintain the security of the Services; to enforce our agreements; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable law.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website uses HubSpot, a service provided by HubSpot, Inc., to measure how the website is used and to manage inquiries and demo requests. The HubSpot tracking code runs only if you select “Accept” in our cookie notice.

If you accept, HubSpot may collect information such as the pages you visit, the dates and times of your visits, the address of the page that referred you, your browser and device type, your IP address, and an identifier stored in a cookie. If you submit a demo request, that browsing information may be linked to the name, email address, company, and other details you provide.

The HubSpot tracking code may set the following cookies:

  • __hstc. Tracks visits, including the first, previous, and current visit. Expires after six months.
  • hubspotutk. Identifies a visitor and is passed to HubSpot when a form is submitted. Expires after six months.
  • __hssc. Keeps track of the current visit. Expires after 30 minutes.
  • __hssrc. Detects the start of a new visit. Expires when you close your browser.

HubSpot may also use browser storage for similar tracking information, and its service providers may set cookies needed for security and traffic management, such as __cf_bm and __cfuvid.

We save your cookie choice in your browser’s local storage for up to six months. You can change it at any time by selecting “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page. Declining removes the HubSpot tracking cookies listed above from this website. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, tracking stays off unless you accept it in the cookie notice.

When you submit a demo request, the details you enter are sent to HubSpot whether or not you accept cookies. After submitting, you may choose to schedule a meeting on a page hosted by HubSpot, which has its own cookies and privacy practices.

Data Security and Retention

We have implemented measures designed to protect personal information from accidental loss and unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention and deletion are subject to applicable law and any governing agreement with VOW. This policy does not establish a fixed deletion period for event information. Deletion requests should be directed to privacy@vow.app.

Privacy Requests and Information About Children

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or exercise other privacy rights. Send requests to privacy@vow.app. Requests concerning organizer-controlled information may require coordination with the organizer. Do not include passwords or authentication credentials.

Our public website is intended for business audiences. Organizers and adults providing information about children are responsible for having the authority and obtaining any consent required by law. A parent or guardian with questions or a request concerning a child’s information may contact privacy@vow.app.

We will post updates to this policy on this page and revise the date above. If we make material changes to how we treat personal information, we will provide notice through the Services and any additional notice required by law.
